About

From the
sales floor
to strategy.

Eight years in cannabis retail — from budtending at Harborside Oakland to eCommerce and operations strategy. I've touched every part of the business: tech stack management, CRM, staffing, marketing, and the messy middle where all of it intersects.

Before cannabis, I spent years learning how a great customer experience actually works — growing up in my mom's hair salon, doing time at Bath & Body Works, matching pets with families at Contra Costa Animal Services. That same empathy-first thinking shapes everything I build for dispensaries today.
